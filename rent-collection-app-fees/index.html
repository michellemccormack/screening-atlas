Rent Collection App Fees Compared: ACH, Card and Payout Times

The subscription price is the number on the pricing page. The number that hits every month is the per-payment fee, who it lands on, and how many days the rent sits in transit. Here are those three numbers for four landlord platforms, from their own pricing and help pages as of November 21, 2026.

The short version. Bank transfer (ACH): TurboTenant $2 per payment on Free and Essentials, typically waived on Pro [TT]; RentRedi $1 [RR]; Rentec Direct $2 per payment on Starter, one free per property per month on Pro/PM [Rentec]; Avail $2.50 on the free plan, no fee on Unlimited Plus [Avail]. Card fees run 2.95% to 3.5%. Standard ACH payout ranges from about 2 business days (RentRedi, Rentec) to 5–7 (TurboTenant without a paid plan).

Things that change the math

Rentec Direct: two of its pages disagree

Rentec's online-payments page says ACH is "free for both parties" [Rentec payments page], and its knowledge base says tenant ACH payments "do not incur a fee for you or the tenant" [Rentec KB]. Its pricing-transparency page charges $2.00 per incoming payment on the Starter plan and applies a fair-usage rate above one ACH per property per month on Pro and PM [Rentec pricing]. Read together, "free" seems to describe the Pro and PM allowance, but confirm with Rentec for your plan our reading. Rentec also says you need a business checking account, because "the banks will not transmit rental payments to personal bank accounts" [source].

TurboTenant: the fee depends on your plan

The renter's ACH fee is $2 unless the landlord is on Pro, where it's typically waived [source]. Faster ACH payouts (2–4 business days) need Essentials or Pro and apply only to eligible payments under $10,000 [source]. TurboTenant explains that expedited payouts advance the money before the renter's ACH has fully cleared [source]. An older TurboTenant FAQ gives card payouts as 2–3 business days rather than 1–3 [older FAQ]; the table uses the October 14, 2026 article.

Who absorbs the fee

If you cover a per-payment fee, it comes out of the rent. On a $1,500 rent paid by ACH, that's $1 a month on RentRedi or $2 on TurboTenant Free/Essentials (both from the fee pages above). Card fees are percentage-based, so on $1,500 a 3.49% fee is $52.35: that's why most platforms push card fees to the tenant.

Some states and cities limit fees a landlord can charge tenants. We have not reviewed whether any state restricts passing payment-processing fees to tenants not yet reviewed.

Monthly software price

For completeness, the subscription each platform publishes: TurboTenant Free $0, Essentials "starting at $12.42/month," Pro "starting at $16.58/month" [source]; RentRedi from $12/month on annual billing, $20 on six-month and $29.95 month-to-month [source]; Rentec Direct Starter $25/month, Pro and PM a $50 monthly minimum at $2 and $2.50 per unit [source]; Avail Unlimited $0/unit, Unlimited Plus $9/unit per month [source].
