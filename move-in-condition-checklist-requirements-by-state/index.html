Move-In Condition Checklists and Photos: Which States Require Them

"That cabinet was already broken when I moved in." Whether a landlord can answer that depends on what was written down on day one, and in several states the paperwork isn't optional. Some states won't let a landlord collect a deposit at all without a signed checklist; others make the move-in report the measuring stick for every later deduction.

The short version. Washington: no deposit without a signed written checklist, and a landlord who collects one without it owes the tenant the deposit [RCW 59.18.260(2),(5)]. Massachusetts: a signed statement of condition within ten days after the tenancy starts [c.186 s.15B(2)(c)]. California: photos at the start of tenancies beginning on or after July 1, 2025 [Cal. Civ. 1950.5(g)(1)]. Virginia: a written move-in report within five days [Va. 55.1-1214(A)]. Arizona and Michigan require a move-in form or checklist [A.R.S. 33-1321(C)] [MCL 554.608]. Minnesota and Wisconsin require notice that the tenant may inspect [Minn. 504B.182] [ATCP 134.06(1)].

Does a tenant have to sign the checklist?

Washington requires both signatures and dates [RCW 59.18.260(3)]. In Massachusetts, the required notice tells the tenant that failing to return the statement within the time limit may be treated by a court as agreement that the list is complete and correct [c.186 s.15B(2)(c)]. Virginia's report is deemed correct unless the tenant objects in writing within five days [Va. 55.1-1214(A)].

Can photos replace a written checklist?

Only where the law says so. Minnesota allows a landlord, with the tenant's agreement, to give written acknowledgment of photos or videos in place of the initial or move-out inspection [Minn. 504B.182 subd. 1(b)]. California requires photos, not a checklist [Cal. Civ. 1950.5(g)]. Washington and Massachusetts require a written checklist or statement [RCW 59.18.260(2)] [c.186 s.15B(2)(c)], so photos are useful extra evidence there but not a substitute.

What landlords should do

  • Use one form for move-in and move-out, as Michigan requires [MCL 554.608(2)]. It makes every deduction a side-by-side comparison.
  • Get it signed and keep a copy with the lease, plus dated photos.
  • Calendar the short deadlines: five days in Virginia, ten days in Massachusetts, 14 days for Minnesota's notice.
  • At move-out, follow your state's return deadline. See security deposit return deadlines by state.
