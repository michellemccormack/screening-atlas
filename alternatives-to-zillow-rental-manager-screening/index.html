Alternatives to Zillow Rental Manager Screening: What Each Includes and Who Pays

Zillow Rental Manager's screening costs the landlord nothing: the applicant pays one fee and the reports come with the application. That suits many landlords, and it is a poor fit for others, mainly those who want to pay for the screening themselves, choose which reports to run, or work in a state that limits what an applicant can be charged. This page lays out what Zillow's service includes and who pays, then how the alternatives differ. Everything is from each provider's own pages as of November 20, 2026.

The short version. On Zillow, the applicant pays $40 and the landlord pays nothing [Zillow]. Apartments.com works the same way at $32 plus tax, and does not let landlords collect their own application fee [Apt.com][Apt.com]. Avail, SmartMove, TurboTenant and RentRedi let the landlord decide who pays, and you choose the reports [Avail][SM][TT][RR].

What Zillow Rental Manager screening includes

  • Cost to landlord: none. "The application and tenant screening reports are free for landlords." [source]
  • Cost to applicant: a $40 application fee that includes the screening reports and lets the renter apply to your listing and to an unlimited number of participating rentals for 30 days. Credit and background information is pulled once during that 30-day period [source].
  • Credit report: from Experian. Zillow lists credit score, open credit lines, employment history, residence history, and past bankruptcies or collections [source].
  • Background check: Zillow's Rental Manager pages say it is performed by CIC and includes a nationwide eviction search, sex offender search, and nationwide criminal search [source]. One of Zillow's own editorial articles says its background checks are run by Checkr [Zillow article]. We could not tell from Zillow's pages which one is current vendor unclear.
  • What the criminal search leaves out: Zillow's help center article says the report includes convictions only, that certain older non-violent felonies and misdemeanors and traffic or vehicle misdemeanors are excluded, and that reports may be modified to reflect state and local law [Zillow help] help page would not load on our last check. The Rental Manager FAQ confirms only that checks "are modified to reflect state and local legal requirements" [source].
  • Customization: "At this time we do not have customizable fields for the rental application." Applicants may upload up to five income documents [source].
  • Other applicants: you can screen applicants who did not come from Zillow by sending an application link [source].

How the alternatives differ from Zillow

You want to pay, or split the cost

On Zillow and Apartments.com the applicant pays and the landlord has no say in it [Zillow][Apt.com]. If you want to cover screening to attract applicants, Avail, SmartMove, TurboTenant and RentPrep let the landlord pay [Avail][SM][TT][RP]. RentRedi's page lists screening as tenant-paid [source].

You want to choose which reports to run

Zillow's $40 is one package. Avail says landlords choose the required reports for each applicant [source], SmartMove sells three tiers [source], and Rentec Direct and RentPrep sell tiered or separate packages [Rentec][RP].

You want an applicant to reuse their report

Zillow's reports can be reused across participating rentals for 30 days, with unlimited applications [source]. Apartments.com allows 30 days and up to 10 participating landlords [source]. If an applicant arrives with a report from elsewhere, some states require you to accept it or waive your fee: see reusable reports by state.

Your state caps what you can charge

A flat applicant fee set by the platform may exceed what your state allows you to collect. Avail's pricing page says the cost of its reports varies by location due to state laws [source]. Zillow says its reports are modified for state and local requirements, but its pages give one $40 fee [source], and we did not find a statement on how that fee is adjusted where a cap is lower not found. Check the fee-cap table before you choose.

Your city requires phased screening

Where criminal history can only be reviewed in a second step (Cook County, NYC, D.C. and others), a bundled application-and-report may not fit. Our comparison covers what SmartMove and TurboTenant each disclose. We did not find a phased-screening statement on Zillow's or Apartments.com's pages not found.
