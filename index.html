Why this site exists

Screening services are sold as "nationwide," but what comes back depends on where the rental is. TransUnion's SmartMoveaffiliate link, for example, publishes a list of the 30 states where its criminal records can currently be returned (SmartMove disclaimer). Landlords often find out about these gaps only after a blank report. We put the rules and the coverage in one place, by state.
