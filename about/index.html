About Screening Atlas

A reference for small landlords and the people applying to rent from them: the rules on screening, and what screening reports actually contain, state by state.

Screening Atlas is an independent comparison guide published by Atlas Guides. For questions, see Contact.

The idea

Comparison sites mostly rank screening tools by price and features. The questions landlords keep asking are about coverage and law: Will this report show criminal records for my county? Can I charge this applicant $50? Must I accept the report they already paid for? The answers change at state lines, so the site is organized by state and by provider.

How we source things

  • Laws are quoted from the state legislature's site where we could reach it. When we use an unofficial compilation (such as Justia or OregonLaws), the table says so.
  • Provider facts (prices, report contents, coverage) come only from that provider's own pricing, help or disclaimer pages.
  • Landlord experiences quoted from forums are labeled as user reports, not facts.
  • Anything we couldn't confirm is marked unverified. A state we haven't reviewed is listed as "not yet reviewed," which says nothing about whether it has a limit.
  • Every page shows the date we last checked it.

What this site is not

It isn't legal advice, and it isn't a screening company. Laws change and local rules can be stricter than state law, so check the linked source or a local attorney before you act.

How the site makes money

We plan to join affiliate programs for some screening and landlord-software providers. None are active today. See the affiliate disclosure. Affiliate status will never change a fact, a price, or the order of a table.
