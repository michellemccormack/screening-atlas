How Long Does Tenant Screening Take? Turnaround and Report Access by Provider

Landlords ask "how long does a background check take?" when a good applicant is waiting and a second one is asking about the unit. The honest answer: the report itself is often quick, and the wait is usually the applicant finishing identity verification. Here is what each provider says about timing, delays, and how long you can see the report afterward.

The short version. SmartMove says it provides most results the same day once the renter confirms and passes identity authentication [SmartMove]. TurboTenant says most checks take 1 to 3 days and some come back the same day [TurboTenant]. On RentRedi, a renter who has to send documents to TransUnion for manual verification can wait 24 to 48 hours [RentRedi]. On Rentec Direct, some reports return in under 60 seconds while county-level criminal searches may take longer [Rentec].

Why is my applicant's screening stuck?

On TransUnion-based platforms, the applicant has to authorize the report and verify their identity themselves. TurboTenant says landlords "cannot answer verification questions or authorize screening on their behalf" [source]. TurboTenant lists the usual causes when a report isn't available: missing landlord mailing address or phone number, the applicant not authorizing, unfinished identity verification, an unpaid fee, an archived applicant, expired verification, and a credit freeze or fraud alert [source]. RentRedi says a "thin file" or a typo in the date of birth or SSN can stop TransUnion from generating questions, and the renter then has to call TransUnion [source].

Applicants without an SSN usually go through manual verification; see screening applicants without an SSN.

How do you speed it up?

  1. Fill in your own mailing address and phone number in your account first. TurboTenant says TransUnion requires them before reports are delivered [source].
  2. Have applicants verify during the application. TurboTenant says applicants who verify during the rental application "get results fastest" [source].
  3. Tell applicants to expect the request email or text, and to call TransUnion right away if the platform tells them to [source].
  4. Download the report as soon as it arrives. SmartMove and RentRedi access ends after 30 days [source] [source].

Does the speed of screening affect fee rules?

It can. In Oregon, if you fill the unit before screening an applicant who paid a screening charge, you must refund it within 30 days [ORS 90.295(5)]. See screening fee refunds when you don't screen.
