Questions

What if the kit isn't what I needed?

There is a 14-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. Email us from the contact page.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is general information and templates based on the cited laws. Cities and counties can add stricter rules, so check your local law, and consider a licensed attorney in your state before relying on any template.

Does it cover my state?

The federal rules (adverse action notices, permissible purpose, fair housing, disposal) apply in every state. The state cheat sheets cover the states and cities named above; if yours isn't listed, the kit shows you what to look for in your own law.

Can I edit the templates?

Yes. The criteria sheet, application, authorization and both adverse action notices come as Word files, and the decision log as an Excel file.

Do I still need a screening service?

Yes. The kit is the paperwork around a screening report, not the report itself. See our screening report comparison to choose one.
